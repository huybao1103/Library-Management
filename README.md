# Topic:

Huflit Library is a library management system that can maximally support librarians in managing all the information of the library. The system also allows students to access the system to register for book loans as well as to view their borrowing history and the status of their library cards.

The system provides management functions for information related to books, authors, publishers, library cards, etc., for librarians along with other utilities that can simplify the process of adding, deleting, and editing for users.

Students can log in to the system using the information on their library cards to view the information of all the books available in the library and to register for borrow books from the library. Students can also view their borrowing history and the status of their library cards.

In addition, the system also needs to have role management for librarians and students assigned to the administrator to secure the library's information.

# Frameworks:

-	**Front-end:** Angular 16.2.1, NodeJS 18.17.1
-	**Back-end:** .NET Core API, .NET SDK 7.0, Visual Studio 2022

# Available features:

- Dashboard
-	Book Management
-	Book Chapter Management
-	Categories Management
-	Authors Management
-	Publishers Management
-	Library Cards Management
-	Borrow History Management
-	Accounts Management
-	Roles & Permissions Management
-	Sign In / Sign Up for reader
-	Book Browsing Page for reader
-	Library Card Information page for reader
-	Book Borrow History records page for reader.
